SC-03 Challenge & Problem Statement

Long and unpredictable queues at service counters, banks, and hospitals waste citizens' time and make it difficult for staff to manage customer demand efficiently.

The Solution: We built an intelligent queue system for Free Palestine Bank to eliminate the frustration of waiting in physical lines without knowing how long it will take. Using a simulated single-counter model, our platform seamlessly manages demand and forecasting.

Role-Based Architecture & Workflow

Counter Operators & Admins

Counter personnel must log in securely. Admins can register additional counter staff. Operators configure the service catalog (defining bank tasks such as Cash Deposit taking 1 minute or Account Opening taking 3 minutes). They oversee the live dashboard to advance tokens to SERVING, COMPLETED, or track user cancellations.

Citizens / Bank Customers

Customers enjoy a completely frictionless experience—no login required. Users simply open the app via web or QR code scan, type their name, select a service, and instantly receive a digital token with live queue position tracking and wait-time countdowns.

Queue Optimization: SJF + Aging Algorithm

To prevent bottlenecks where fast transactions get stuck behind lengthy requests, our backend uses Shortest Job First (SJF) combined with an aging factor:

Priority Score = ServiceTime - (WaitTime * 0.2)

Service durations defined by the banker during job creation directly feed this algorithm, allowing accurate wait-time estimation and fair queue reordering.
